## Frequently asked questions

##### What is an AI Teammate?

An AI Teammate is a collaborative AI agent you assign work to like a colleague. Unlike a chatbot, it acts on shared project context, works inside real workflows, and delivers outcomes — with checkpoints for human review.

##### How are AI Teammates different from an AI assistant or chatbot?

Most AI tools are single-player — one person prompting a model in a private tab. Asana AI Teammates are multiplayer: they live in your projects, draw context from the Asana Work Graph®, and build shared memory of how your team works, so they take on work end to end rather than just answering questions.

##### What can I assign to an AI Teammate?

You can assign an AI Teammate work like a colleague — drafting content, triaging and routing requests, summarizing status, reviewing work, and coordinating cross-functional tasks. They break work into subtasks and leave comments as they go, so your team can review or redirect at any checkpoint.

##### Can I build my own AI Teammate?

Yes. You can start from 30+ prebuilt AI Teammates for marketing, ops, and IT, healthcare, manufacturing, retail, and professional services, or use the no-code builder to design your own — defining its role, permissions, and responsibilities. Either way, no prompt engineering is required. Learn [how to build or find an AI Teammate](https://help.asana.com/s/article/how-to-create-or-discover-an-ai-teammate).

##### How do I stay in control of what AI Teammates do?

Every AI Teammate has an identity, scoped permissions, spend controls, and an audit trail, and every action is checkpointable and reversible. They can only access what the triggering user already has permission to see — so IT, Legal, and Finance can say yes. Learn more about [Asana AI features and admin controls](https://help.asana.com/s/article/asana-ai-features-and-admin-controls?language=en_US).

##### Are AI Teammates available on every Asana plan?

AI Teammates are available on Starter, Advanced, Enterprise, and Enterprise+ plans. They aren't included in the free Personal plan.

##### Do I need an add-on to use AI Teammates?

AI Teammates are available automatically as part of all paid plans. AI Teammates use AI requests and you can add more AI requests as needed. A live usage dashboard and spend limits keep costs predictable. Explore [Asana's plans and pricing](https://asana.com/pricing) to see what's included in each plan.

##### What's the difference between AI Teammates and AI Studio?

Both are part of [Agentic Work Management](https://asana.com/product/ai). AI Studio is a [no-code builder](https://asana.com/product/ai/ai-studio) for the repetitive, structured work that slows teams down — intake, routing, approvals, status updates — while AI Teammates are agents with the skills to take on work alongside your team. Many teams use them together.

##### What's the difference between AI Teammates and Dash?

Asana Dash is your [AI Chief of Staff](https://asana.com/product/ai/dash) — it knows your goals and priorities, surfaces what needs your attention, and routes work to the right AI Teammate. AI Teammates are the agents that carry out the work, so together they help human-agent teams move critical work from plan to outcome.
